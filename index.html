What is Cryptocurrency Exchange Script

A White Label Cryptocurrency Exchange Script is a customizable pre-built software solution that lets entrepreneurs and business persons to launch their own cryptocurrency trading platform instantly without the need of developing it from scratch. It has essential features like futures trading and spot, KYC/AML compliance, multi-currency-support, liquid management support as well as other top-tier security protocols. By using this ready made software, it helps the companies to diminish the development cost by ensuring a scalable, safe, secure and high-performance trading platform. On the other hand, it supports several trading models like Decentralized (DEX), Centralized (CEX), and Peer-To-Peer (P2P) exchanges, that acts as a functional solution for the entrepreneurs, who desires to enter into the blooming cryptocurrency market. .
